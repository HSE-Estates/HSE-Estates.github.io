Room Scanner
Camera to floor plan

Surveys

Stand in the middle of a room, tap each corner where the wall meets the floor, and get a dimensioned, editable floor plan. Everything is measured on the device and stored on the device.

Scanning needs a camera and a motion sensor. That means a phone or tablet, served over https or from localhost — browsers block the camera otherwise. On a desktop you can still draw, edit, style and export plans by hand.

How the measuring works

Your phone knows which way it is pointing. When you tap a corner, that tap becomes a ray in space; the floor is a known plane below you; where the two meet is a real coordinate in metres. No reconstruction, no upload, no waiting. The one unknown is how high you are holding the phone — and because every point scales with it by the same factor, measuring a single wall with a tape corrects the entire room at once.
