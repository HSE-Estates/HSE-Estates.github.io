    Step by step

      Every building sits on the site plan at a known position, so the campus already has the right shape. Real coordinates anchor that shape to the world. Two anchors are enough, and every extra one you add under Buildings tightens the fit for all the rest. The two references below are only used until two buildings have exact coordinates of their own.

      Tap a building to rename it, change what is listed on its card, add hidden search terms, or set its exact coordinate. Pin sets the coordinate straight away.

      Every pin measured against the building outlines in OpenStreetMap, and against Google where Google names the place. Google opens that exact coordinate with a pin in a new tab: switch Google to Satellite, and if the pin is not on the right roof, come back and use Move to drag it here. Tap a row to fly the map to it.

      The network is the real one: every campus road, footpath, crossing and set of steps from OpenStreetMap, plus the pavements on Douglas Road and South Douglas Road. Routes never pass through a building outline.

      Where you know a path that is missing, such as a gap in a wall or a cut through a car park, turn Draw on and tap once on each path it should join: taps on or beside an existing path snap onto it. Record drops a point every 12 m as you walk. Move drags points into place and Erase removes the point you tap (zoom right in to see them). Tick step-free off before drawing a link with steps.

      Print a sign or QR code at each entrance using these links. Opening one sets the visitor's starting point, so the directions are right even with no GPS signal.

      One row per building: block, name, latitude, longitude. Tabs or commas both work. Rows that match an existing block update it, new block numbers are added.

      70 m/min is a steady older-adult walking pace. Lower it for a campus with hills or long corridors.

      The app ships with keyless sources that allow third party use. If you take out a map account, paste its tile URL here with the key in it and it becomes the first choice. Anything you paste is visible to anyone who opens the app, so lock the key to your own domain in the provider's dashboard.

      The header pulls the HSE Estates logo straight from your GitHub repo. If that ever goes offline it falls back to a file named hse-logo.png sitting beside this one, and then to a plain text lockup, so the header never breaks.
